Better access. Stronger communities.

A proposal to make behavioral health support easier to reach.

Three opportunities for change

  • Care close to home

    Offering counseling in schools and community centers.

  • Why it helps

    Familiar locations and fewer transportation barriers.

  • Challenges

    Funding qualified staff, private rooms, scheduling

  • Guidance at every step

    A care navigator helps people find providers, book appointments and follow up.

  • Why it helps

    Clearer referrals and support through the process.

  • Challenges

    Staff training, provider availability, confidentially.

  • Care that fits your life

    Offer evening and weekend visits, affordable fees, and virtual options.

  • Why it helps

    More choices for different schedules and budgets.

  • Challenges

    Staffing, sustainable funding, internet access, privacy.

One connected community program

Local counseling + referral support + flexible appointments
